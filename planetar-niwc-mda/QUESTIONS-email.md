**Subject:** N66001-26-S-C036 — questions before a solution brief (Zax Analytics, Canada)

Mr. Mansfield,

Zax Analytics is a Canadian company preparing a Step-1 solution brief for CSO N66001-26-S-C036,
Data and Analytics for Maritime Domain Awareness. Our offer is a dark-vessel candidate feed built
on open data, where every result names the specific sources it was derived from (the Sentinel-1
scene, the AIS feed) and the method is published in full. Before we write, we would be grateful
for answers to the following:

1. **SeaVision ingest.** Is there a preferred format or interface for third-party feeds into
   SeaVision (for example a specific JSON schema, GeoJSON, or a particular REST pattern), or a
   document describing it that vendors may receive?
2. **Status of the CSO.** Are solution briefs still being evaluated and funded, and are briefs
   received between now and 7 January 2027 evaluated as they arrive?
3. **Foreign vendor, Prototype OT.** Is a Prototype Other Transaction realistically available to a
   first-time Canadian vendor, and are there clearance, facility or licence requirements we should
   expect for an unclassified data-and-analytics feed?
4. **SAM.gov timing.** We are starting NCAGE and SAM.gov registration now. May we submit a brief
   while registration is in progress, given §6.1 requires it before award?
5. **Market success (§4.1 item 7).** For an early-stage company, what evidence of market traction
   is most useful to you — pilots, public benchmarks, open-source adoption?
6. **Data rights.** Our software is open source (GPLv3) and our inputs are open data. Are there
   data-rights or licensing terms the Navy expects for delivered analytical results that we should
   address in the brief?

Thank you for your time.
