# CSA smartEarth — program facts

Research note on the Canadian Space Agency's **smartEarth** initiative (satellite-EO
applications development). Compiled 2026-07-02. ⚠️ The CSA website was unreachable
during verification — figures below are from CSA secondary pages and press coverage;
**re-verify on asc-csa.gc.ca before acting.**

## At a glance

| Field | Value | Source |
|---|---|---|
| Program | **smartEarth** — "smart use of satellite data to develop solutions to key challenges on Earth" | CSA |
| Mechanism | Periodic **Announcements of Opportunity (AO)** and RFPs; contributions typically 1–3 years | CSA |
| Tracks | **Accelerator**, **Integrator**, **Enabler** (funding varies by AO) | CSA |
| Recent scale | Biodiversity AO (launched 2025-01): **20 companies at up to $250K each**; an earlier round awarded **$8M to 21 companies** | CSA / SpaceQ |
| FY 2026-27 plan | **~$2.65M** planned smartEarth investment | CSA Departmental Plan 2026-27 |
| Open AO today? | **None confirmed as of 2026-07-02** (site unreachable during check) | — |
| Eligibility | Historically Canadian for-profit organizations (and academia, per AO terms) — **re-verify per AO** | CSA award records |

## planetar fit

- **The pitch is already built:** `planetar-sat` (Sentinel-1 SAR: CDSE fetch → CFAR
  detect → IoU track → bus) fused with AIS/EO/acoustic feeds; live demo at planetar.ca.
- **EO application, civilian face (D3):** dark-vessel detection for IUU fishing, Arctic
  shipping safety, and pollution response — squarely "solutions to key challenges on
  Earth using satellite data."
- **Canadian angle:** RADARSAT Constellation Mission (RCM) data uptake is a recurring CSA
  priority; adding RCM alongside Sentinel-1 is a natural AO workplan. Related precedent
  to research: Canada's Dark Vessel Detection program (DFO, satellite-based) —
  *unverified, check before citing*.
- **Vehicle:** Zax Analytics (D1/C1 — Steven as CEO performing the R&D).
- **Fit filter:** software/data deliverable ✓; TRL band varies by AO — screen each.
