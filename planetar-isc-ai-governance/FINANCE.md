# FINANCE — the financial-capability check, cash flow, and Q9 (2026-11-05)

Three separate things, often confused:

| | What | When | Scored? |
|---|---|---|---|
| **Financial proposal** | Budget table in the submission form: labour hours × firm rates (inclusive of overhead and profit), materials, subcontracts, other direct costs | At submission | Must not exceed $2M; amounts above it count as co-investment (CFP §3.4.1) |
| **Q9: financial controls** | How the funds will be managed, tracked and reported, and by whom (with credentials) | At submission | 10 points |
| **Financial capability check** | Whether Zax can afford to carry the work until Canada pays | After selection, before award (CFP §4.5.2) | Pass/fail; failing it means no contract |

## What the check can ask for (CFP §4.5.2)

"May request", so not automatic: audited or unaudited financial statements; balance sheet; statement
of retained earnings; income statement; **detailed monthly cash-flow statement**; **bank balance**;
certification by an authorized signing officer; "any other financial-related documents". "If an
Offeror fails to demonstrate adequate financial resources to complete the Work, a contract will not be
awarded."

## Why cash flow is the risk: how Canada pays (CFP Att. 6 §9–10)

- **Payment arrives after the work.** Either *progress payments* (claimed for costs incurred, "no more
  than once a month", up to a negotiated percentage, so a **holdback** may sit until the end), or
  *milestone payments* (only after Canada **accepts** all the work of the milestone, §10.1). Claims may
  not be submitted until the work in them is complete (§9.2d).
- **Canada pays within 30 days** of an acceptable invoice (§9.3). If the invoice is non-conforming it is
  bounced within 15 days and the 30 days restart, so the realistic lag is 30–45 days or more.
- **Which method applies is negotiated with the Statement of Work** (§4.5.3). It is our main lever.

### Peak cash Zax must carry (model, not a quote)

Flat spend over 20 months, costs paid as incurred, invoice at the end of each period, paid 30 days
later; the holdback is released about a month after final acceptance. The last column adds the 15-day
bounce.

| Budget (burn) | Monthly progress, no holdback | Monthly, 10% holdback | Milestone every 2 months | every 3 months | every 4 months | every 4 months, +15 days |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| $0.75M ($38K/month) | $71K | $139K | $109K | $146K | $184K | $202K |
| $1.00M ($50K/month) | $95K | $185K | $145K | $195K | $245K | $270K |
| $1.50M ($75K/month) | $143K | $278K | $218K | $293K | $367K | $405K |
| **$2.00M ($100K/month)** | **$190K** | **$370K** | **$290K** | **$390K** | **$490K** | **$540K** |

Reading it: on a full $2M contract, Zax needs roughly **$190K–$540K of cash or credit** available
throughout, depending almost entirely on the payment method. Monthly progress payments with no holdback
halve the requirement compared with milestones every three months. The model ignores taxes, the delay
before the first payment can be claimed, and any acceptance disputes, so treat it as a floor.

## Ways to pass the check (all founder decisions)

1. **Negotiate monthly progress payments, and the lowest holdback possible.** The single biggest lever.
2. **Short milestones** if milestones are imposed: two months, not four.
3. **Size the ask to the cash you can show.** The pass condition is "adequate financial resources", so
   $1.0–1.5M with a credible cash position beats $2M without one. Nothing requires asking for the
   maximum, and a smaller budget also eases Q6 (plan feasibility) and Q7 (team).
4. **Show a backstop:** cash on hand, a business line of credit or a shareholder loan commitment, sized
   to the peak above. Arrange it before the gate on 2026-12-15, since award comes months later but the
   bid should not promise what cannot be shown.
5. **Bookkeeping that produces the documents quickly:** monthly statements and a 20-month cash-flow
   forecast in the CFP's format. An accountant who prepares them also strengthens Q9.

## Q9 draft skeleton (10 points; fill the `[TODO founder]` items)

> Zax Analytics will manage Phase 2 funds through [accounting system, e.g. QuickBooks Online] with a
> project code per milestone and cost category (labour, subcontracts, other direct costs), so every
> claim on form PWGSC-TPSGC 1111 traces to recorded costs. [Name/firm, credentials] prepares monthly
> statements and a rolling actual-versus-forecast report against the approved budget; variances over
> [10]% trigger a review by the CEO and, where they affect scope, a discussion with the Technical
> Authority before costs are incurred. Labour is recorded in timesheets against tasks of the Statement
> of Work. Subcontracts are fixed-price per deliverable, with payment only on acceptance. Records are
> kept for [six] years for government audit (Contract Cost Principle 1031-2). [Line of credit or cash
> reserve of $X] covers costs between progress payments.

Points come from specifics: a named system, a named person or firm with credentials, a reporting
cadence, a variance threshold, and audit readiness.
