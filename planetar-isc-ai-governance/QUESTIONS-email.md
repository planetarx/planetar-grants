Hello,

On behalf of Zax Analytics, we have the following questions about the Shared Services Canada
challenge "Artificial Intelligence (AI) decision-making process visibility, traceability and
compliance governance":

1. **Relevant environment for TRL 5.** Would validation against publicly available Government of
   Canada governance data — the Government of Canada AI Register and published Algorithmic Impact
   Assessments, assessed against the current Directive on Automated Decision-Making and Policy on
   Service and Digital — together with continuous monitoring of a deployed AI model, be
   considered a relevant environment for demonstrating TRL 5?
2. **Subcontracting.** Is there a maximum share of the Phase 2 budget that may be subcontracted?
3. **Interoperability standards.** Does SSC have preferred open standards for exchanging controls
   and assessment results (e.g. NIST OSCAL), or particular AI/ML lifecycle tools it expects
   connectors for?
4. **Test environment.** Will the selected contractor(s) have access to an SSC test environment
   or sample governance records during Phase 2, or should the prototype assume public data only?
