Subject: Enquiry — ISC Call 004 (EN578-24ISC4), SSC challenge "AI decision-making process visibility, traceability and compliance governance" (CB-189-51083224)

Hello,

On behalf of Zax Analytics, we have the following questions about the Shared Services Canada
challenge "Artificial Intelligence (AI) decision-making process visibility, traceability and
compliance governance" (Challenge Notice CB-189-51083224, under Call for Proposals 004, EN578-24ISC4):

1. **Relevant environment for TRL 5 (Attachment 2, Question 2).** Would validation against publicly
   available Government of Canada governance data — the Government of Canada AI Register and the
   Algorithmic Impact Assessments published on the Open Government Portal, assessed against the
   current Directive on Automated Decision-Making and Policy on Service and Digital — together with
   continuous monitoring of a deployed AI model, be considered a relevant environment for
   demonstrating TRL 5?
2. **Governance instruments in scope (Challenge Notice, Essential Outcome 4A).** Which documents
   should be treated as the "AI Strategy" and "AI ethics and responsibilities" for the minimum
   assessment coverage — for example, the AI Strategy for the Federal Public Service 2025–2027 and
   the Guide on the use of generative artificial intelligence — or does SSC expect others?
3. **Interoperability standards (Challenge Notice, Essential Outcomes 2A–2C).** Does SSC have
   preferred open standards for exchanging controls and assessment results (for example NIST OSCAL),
   or particular enterprise systems or AI/ML lifecycle tools it expects connectors for?
4. **Test environment (Challenge Notice, Essential Outcomes 2B and 6C).** Will the selected
   contractor have access to an SSC test environment or sample governance records during Phase 2,
   or should the prototype assume public data only?
