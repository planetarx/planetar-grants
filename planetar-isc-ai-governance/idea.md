> **Weak magnetic anomaly detection for defence and security**
> Challenge sponsor: Innovation, Science and Economic Development Canada (ISED)
> Closing date: November 27, 2026, 14:00 Eastern time
> ISED is seeking a novel magnetometer (either quantum or classical or a combination thereof) that
> provides advantage over existing capabilities either in sensitivity, specificity, or Size, Weight,
> Power and Cost (SWaP-C), and multi-domain employability.
> This funding opportunity is exclusively open to Phase 2 (Prototype Development) proposals. Only
> solutions that fall within Technology Readiness Level 5 to 9 are eligible to be submitted.
>
> **Artificial Intelligence (AI) decision-making process visibility, traceability and compliance governance**
> Challenge sponsor: Shared Services Canada (SSC)
> Closing date: December 22, 2026, 14:00 Eastern time
> SSC is interested in solutions that provide visibility into AI decision-making processes, enhance
> the traceability of AI outputs, and ensure that AI models comply with the Federal Government's AI
> Strategy and its Guiding Principles.
> This funding opportunity is exclusively open to Phase 2 (Prototype Development) proposals. Only
> solutions that fall within Technology Readiness Level 5 to 9 are eligible to be submitted.
>
> **Strengthening Canada's Nitrogen-Vacancy Diamond Capability**
> Challenge sponsor: Innovation, Sciences and Economic Development Canada (ISED)
> Closing date: November 27, 2026, 14:00 Eastern time
> The Government of Canada is seeking to strengthen Canada's industrial capacity and expertise in
> nitrogen vacancy (NV) diamond materials to support the growth of quantum sensing technologies.
> This funding opportunity is open to Phase 1 (Proof of Feasibility) and Phase 2 (Prototype
> Development) proposals.
>
> Submit your proposal and you could get up to $2.3M to fund the development of your solution!
