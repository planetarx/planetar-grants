## What it is

NATO's **Defence Innovation Accelerator for the North Atlantic (DIANA)** runs an annual
challenge-based accelerator for **dual-use deep tech** across the 32 NATO nations. The **2027
cohort** call is **open now**. One of the six 2027 challenges is a near-exact description of
planetar:

**"Multidomain Sensing and Advanced Data Processing for Intelligence and Surveillance"** —
improve situational awareness through advanced sensing, **data fusion** and analytics.
Per the published challenge text, solutions should include:

- **Sensor and data fusion** technologies to integrate data from **various platforms**
  (autonomous platforms, satellites, ground sensors) and from **different sources, formats,
  and security classifications**;
- **Analytics, algorithms, AI and causal models** to process and interpret large volumes of
  data, providing **actionable and causality-based intelligence and predictive insights**.

(Sibling 2027 challenges include human survivability, multidomain autonomy of uncrewed systems,
operational resilience in contested environments, responsive logistics, and scalable air-defence
countermeasures. The 2026 round ran a related "Decision Superiority for NATO Warfighters" /
"Data Assisted Decision Making" challenge — *that one leaned higher-maturity; this 2027 ISR
challenge is the better-fit, more open one.*)

## What you submit

**Application portal: https://proposals.diana.tech/** (the `diana.nato.int` "Apply" button routes
here; the old `nato-diana.org` is challenge-info only). **Registration prerequisites — sort these
first:**
- ⚠️ **DUNS number required to register *and* submit — UEI is no longer accepted.** **Check the DUNS
  lookup first** (an established company like Zax may already have one → instant unblock):
  https://www.dnb.com/duns-number/lookup.html. Otherwise request one from D&B (can take several days).
- **2FA**: account is registered against an authenticator app (QR code at sign-up).

Multi-step application:

1. **Business identification** — company info; select the challenge.
2. **Short-form proposal** — scored on technical criteria: alignment to challenge, validity,
   feasibility, **dual-use potential**, coherence.
3. **Long-form proposal** — a **1-page Quad Chart + 4-page proposal** describing the technology
   and dual-use applications. **You must use DIANA's Quad Chart + Long-Form templates and follow
   their font/formatting rules — non-compliant applications are rejected.** Scored on:
   - **Technical** — alignment to challenge, **novelty**, feasibility, validity
   - **Defence & security** — relevance, impact on NATO capabilities
   - **Commercial & adoption** — market viability, commercial/adoption approach
   - **General** — resources & dependencies, **DIANA suitability**
4. **Pitch** — a **30-minute online panel**: 10-min presentation + 20-min Q&A (Stage-2 panel adds
   DIANA's trusted-investor network).

> ⚠️ **Format compliance:** our `proposal/` drafts hold the *content*, structured to the criteria
> above. Before submission, **download the official Quad Chart + Long-Form templates from the
> portal and paste our content into them**, obeying their page/font limits. Do not submit our
> Markdown as-is.

## What you get

- **€100,000** in contractual (non-dilutive, grant-style) funding for the accelerator phase;
- Access to **16+ accelerator sites and 200+ test centres** across Europe and North America,
  plus mentors, military end-users, industry partners, and a trusted-investor network;
- Top performers: **up to €300,000** more in the next ("Mission Track") phase;
- A NATO credibility stamp no Canadian grant can match.

**Phases:** Innovator selection → Accelerator programme → Down-selection → Mission Track →
Adoption pathways. (Public sources don't fix per-phase durations for 2027 — **confirm the
program calendar on the portal**; expect a multi-month accelerator commitment, the binding
bandwidth cost for a solo founder.)
