# Emergent Ventures — program facts

Research note on Emergent Ventures (EV). Verified 2026-07-02 against mercatus.org and
secondary coverage.

## At a glance

| Field | Value | Source |
|---|---|---|
| Funder | **Emergent Ventures** — Mercatus Center, George Mason University; administered by Tyler Cowen | mercatus.org |
| What | Fellowships + grants for "highly scalable, zero-to-one ideas for meaningfully improving society" | mercatus.org |
| Grant size | **US$1,000–$50,000** (typical grants; fellowships also exist) | secondary coverage |
| Cadence | **Rolling** — apply anytime; decisions typically fast (weeks) | mercatus.org / coverage |
| Eligible | **Individuals, 13+, worldwide**; no incorporation needed | mercatus.org |
| Application | Short web form — low overhead by design ("fast and lightweight… no rigid requirements") | mercatus.org · form: mercatus.tfaforms.net/5099527 |
| Style | Rewards unusual, ambitious, concrete bets on people; known for funding solo builders | program history |
