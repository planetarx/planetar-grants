## The four base requirements

To be eligible for IRAP **financial assistance**, the applicant must:

1. **Be incorporated** — a for-profit corporation. *(Hard requirement. See the gap below.)*
2. **Be for-profit and operating in Canada.**
3. **Employ ≤ 500 people** (full-time-equivalent). Micro-firms qualify; there is no published
   *minimum* employee count, but see "the capacity test" below.
4. **Develop and commercialize innovative, technology-driven products or services**, with a
   credible path to economic benefit **in Canada**.

You'll also need: a **CRA business number**, a **business plan**, **recent financial
statements**, **ownership-structure** details, and **management/technical team credentials**.

## The structure rule (now satisfied)

NRC IRAP **explicitly does not support** any of these structures:

- **Sole proprietorships**
- **Partnerships**
- **Cooperatives**
- **Unlimited liability corporations (ULCs)**
- **Limited liability corporations (LLCs)** *(US-style LLCs)*

**Zax Analytics is an incorporated Canadian corporation (since ~2015)** → it is on the right
side of this rule. The work sits inside the corporation, as required. Nothing to do here.

## The "capacity test" — the real bar for a solo founder

Meeting the four requirements gets you in the door; it does **not** guarantee funding. NRC
assesses applicants **holistically** on:

- **Technical innovation** — is the work genuinely novel/non-trivial R&D? *(planetar: yes —
  learned cross-modal fusion; strong.)*
- **Management, business & financial capacity** — **can this organization actually execute
  the project?** For a **solo founder this is the crux** — but planetar is better positioned
  than most: **a ~10-year incorporated operating history** is itself evidence of a stable,
  managed business, which is precisely what an ITA wants to see on the financial/management
  axis. Further mitigants that help:
  - The **decade of corporate financials** — show stability and that the company can carry
    its cost-share and manage a contribution.
  - A **smaller first project** (Accelerated Review <$50K) to prove delivery on an IRAP file.
  - A **YEP hire** (a funded young graduate) so the project isn't single-threaded on you.
  - Evidence of **already-built, working code** (the planetar services) and a **filed,
    competitive IDEaS bid** — concrete proof you ship.
  - Naming **subcontractors** for parts you won't do solo (IRAP funds up to 50% of these).
- **Likelihood of technical & commercial success** + **commercialization plan** + **benefit
  to Canada.**
