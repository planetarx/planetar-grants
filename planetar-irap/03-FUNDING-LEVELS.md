# 03 — Funding levels, cost-sharing & eligible costs

> Numbers below are from NRC and reputable IRAP advisory guides (2026). Exact caps are set
> per-project by your ITA and shift with program budgets — **treat these as planning ranges,
> confirm with the ITA.** Sources in [`07-SOURCES.md`](07-SOURCES.md).

## Project tiers (by size & review path)

| Tier | Typical size | Review path | Notes |
|---|---|---|---|
| **Small / Accelerated Review (ARP)** | **< $50,000** | Fast-tracked | Best **on-ramp** for a first-time, solo applicant. Lower scrutiny, quick decision. |
| **Regular / Core IRAP** | **$50,000 – $250,000** | Standard ITA-led | The main band; first-time applicants usually land **$75K–$200K**. |
| **Large (Large Value Contribution, LVC)** | up to **$10,000,000** | Senior review | Multi-year, scale-up firms; not a realistic first ask for a solo micro-firm. |

- **Annual cap signal:** contributions are commonly framed as **up to 75% of eligible cost
  or ~$1,000,000/year**, whichever binds.
- **Program-wide average award** (FY2024-25): **~$125K/firm**.

## Cost-sharing rates (the "80 / 50 / 75" rule)

IRAP is a **cost-shared contribution** — it pays a *percentage*, you fund the rest:

- **Up to 80%** of **eligible salaries** for your **technical staff** working on the project.
- **Up to 50%** of **eligible contractor / subcontractor** costs.
- **Total government assistance capped at ~75%** of the overall project cost (this cap also
  governs **stacking** with other government programs — you can't have IRAP + another federal
  program push public funding past the cap on the *same* costs).

**Worked example (illustrative, Core IRAP):**
> A $200K project: ~$160K technical salaries + $40K subcontracted work.
> IRAP could contribute up to **80% × $160K = $128K** of salaries **+ 50% × $40K = $20K** of
> subcontracts = **$148K**, but the **75% total cap** trims it to **≤ $150K** — so roughly
> **$148K IRAP / $52K applicant**. Real figures are negotiated with the ITA.

## What costs are eligible

- ✅ **Salaries/wages of technical staff** directly performing the R&D (the main bucket).
- ✅ **Subcontractor / consultant** technical work (up to 50%).
- ⚠️ Some **materials** and project-specific costs may qualify case-by-case — confirm.
- ❌ **Not eligible:** routine operating costs, general overhead/admin, general marketing,
  capital equipment as a rule, offshore work, anything without a clear R&D + commercialization
  link.

**Implication for a solo founder:** IRAP pays a % of *salaries*. To draw meaningfully, the
company generally needs to be **paying T4 salary** for the technical work (including,
typically, the founder's own technical labour once incorporated and on payroll) and/or
**hiring** (where YEP and Core IRAP combine well). This is another reason incorporation +
payroll setup is foundational.

## Stream-specific amounts (AI Assist / DI Assist)

The dedicated **AI Assist** and **DI Assist** program pages **do not publish fixed award
sizes** — they run *through* the same ITA/contribution machinery, so the tiers and 80/50/75
rates above apply. The headline numbers ($100M AI Assist, $244.2M DI Assist) are the
**program envelopes**, not per-company awards. Get the live per-project guidance from the ITA.

## Decision timelines (for budgeting your runway)

- **YEP / small hires:** ~**2 weeks** to a decision.
- **Funding decisions generally:** ~**20–65 business days**, scaling with the amount requested.
- **First-time end-to-end** (cold call → assigned ITA → scoped → approved): realistically
  **3–6 months**. Plan runway accordingly; this is **not** fast money on the first project.
