# DECISION — Hold IRAP until cash flow (2026-06-30)

> **Status: ⏸️ ON HOLD — not pursuing IRAP now.** Decided 2026-06-30 by Steven Ness.
> Revisit when Zax Analytics has cash flow. Research is complete and parked; nothing is
> blocked on missing information — only on the company's financial readiness.

## The decision

**Do not engage NRC IRAP yet.** Complete the research (done), keep the business plan ready
(done), and **wait to make the first call until the company has revenue / cash flow.**

## Why (one paragraph)

IRAP is structurally a strong fit for planetar — it's non-repayable, planetar lands in **two**
priority streams (AI Assist + DI Assist), and the eligibility gate (incorporation) is cleared.
**But IRAP reimburses *paid* technical salary** — up to 80%, after the cost is incurred, and
never 100%. Zax Analytics is **pre-revenue with no payroll and no working capital**, so today
there is no salary to cost-share against and no cash to (a) float the reimbursement gap or
(b) fund the company's own ~20–25% share. That makes the financing structure — not the R&D —
the binding constraint. The clean fix is **cash flow first, IRAP second**: once there is
revenue to put the founder on T4 payroll and float the gap, the same well-scoped fusion-model
R&D becomes straightforwardly fundable.

## What is NOT the reason for the hold

To keep the record honest — the hold is **purely financial readiness**, not a fit problem:

- ✅ **Eligibility:** cleared (incorporated Canadian for-profit since ~2015).
- ✅ **Program fit:** strong — AI Assist (deep learning) + DI Assist (defence dual-use).
- ✅ **Technical merit / capacity evidence:** strong (shipped ~13k-LOC substrate, filed IDEaS
  bid, published semi-supervised-ML record).
- ✅ **Business plan:** drafted and ready (`business-plan/BUSINESS-PLAN.md`).
- ⏸️ **Financial readiness:** the only gap — pre-revenue, no payroll, no working capital.

## Re-engage trigger (when to take this off hold)

Pick the call back up when **any** of these is true:

1. **Recurring revenue / cash flow** exists at Zax Analytics sufficient to (a) put the founder
   on **T4 payroll** and (b) float the IRAP reimbursement gap + the ~20–25% company share.
2. **The IDEaS CH13 award lands** (CP6-132484, pending; eval ~3–6 mo from 2026-06-01 → decision
   roughly Q3–Q4 2026). That award would itself be working capital — though note IRAP and
   IDEaS **cannot double-fund the same work**, so an IRAP project would have to cover
   **different scope or a different period**.
3. A **founder capital injection or small bridge** is put in specifically to seed payroll.
