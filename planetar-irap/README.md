## TL;DR

- **IRAP is the single best-fit Canadian funding program for planetar after IDEaS.** It is
  a **non-repayable** wage-subsidy program for R&D in Canadian tech SMEs. It covers **up to
  80% of technical-staff salaries** and **up to 50% of subcontractors**, total assistance
  capped at **75%** of project cost. First-time awards typically land **$75K–$200K**.
- **Two streams map directly onto planetar:**
  - **AI Assist** — funds GenAI / **deep-learning** R&D. planetar's learned cross-modal
    fusion model *is* a deep-learning system. ($100M, Budget 2024, over 5 yrs.)
  - **DI Assist** — funds **defence & dual-use** R&D addressing CAF capability needs;
    named target areas include **surveillance** and **autonomous systems**. planetar's
    maritime dark-vessel detection is squarely dual-use. ($244.2M, starting FY2025-26.)
- **✅ Eligibility cleared on structure.** IRAP funds **only incorporated, for-profit
  corporations** (sole props / partnerships / LLCs / ULCs are ineligible). **Zax Analytics
  has been incorporated since ~2015** — a ~10-year history that not only clears the gate but
  *strengthens* the financial/management-capacity case that's usually the soft spot for solo
  founders. A **DUNS number** is in progress (useful for the defence/DI Assist track, though
  not an IRAP requirement). See [`02-ELIGIBILITY.md`](02-ELIGIBILITY.md).
- **It is relationship-driven, not a portal deadline.** There is no open call to "submit"
  against. You phone NRC, get assigned an **Industrial Technology Advisor (ITA)**, and the
  ITA co-develops and sponsors the proposal internally. **One call starts everything:**
  **1-877-994-4727**.
