# 04 — How to apply (the ITA-led process)

> **Mental model:** IRAP is **not** a portal with an open call and a submit button (the way
> IDEaS CFP6 was). There is no deadline to race. It is a **relationship**: you get an
> advisor, you co-develop a project with them, and they sponsor it internally. The whole
> thing starts with **one phone call**.

## The steps

### 1. Call the contact centre — **1-877-994-4727**
Bilingual, toll-free. A **senior person from the company** should make the call (NRC wants to
talk to a decision-maker, not an intermediary). They gather basic details and decide whether
you're ready to be matched. *(You can also use the "Contact us / find out if you're eligible"
form on the NRC IRAP site, but the call is faster.)*

### 2. Meet a Client Engagement Advisor (CEA)
The CEA does an initial screen — are you incorporated, for-profit, doing real tech R&D, ready
to execute? If yes, they route you to a regional/sector **ITA**.

### 3. Get assigned an Industrial Technology Advisor (ITA)
Your dedicated advisor for **Vancouver Island / Victoria**. (NRC won't publish a named local
ITA online — the toll-free line assigns one for your region. 250+ ITAs across 130 offices.)
**Invest in this relationship** — the ITA is mentor, gatekeeper, and internal champion.

### 4. Co-develop the project proposal with the ITA
Together you define **scope, technical milestones, timeline, budget, and the
commercialization plan.** The ITA shapes it to fit what IRAP can fund and what will pass
internal review. The proposal must show **technical innovation + feasibility + capacity to
execute + benefit to Canada.**

### 5. Submit / approval
Submit through the NRC IRAP portal as directed by the ITA, with all attachments. Decision in
~**20–65 business days** (amount-dependent; small/YEP ~2 weeks).

### 6. Execute & claim
Run the project; **claim reimbursement against actual eligible salary/subcontractor costs**
(it's a reimbursement model — you spend, document, then claim the contribution %). Keep
clean, project-tagged **timesheets and financial records** — claims and any audit depend on
them.

## Documents to have ready (assemble before the call where possible)

| Document | planetar source / status |
|---|---|
| **CRA business number** | Verify exists (tied to incorporation) |
| **Certificate of incorporation** | **Blocker — confirm/obtain** (see [`02-ELIGIBILITY.md`](02-ELIGIBILITY.md)) |
| **Business plan** | Draft from existing proposal + market docs (`../../planetar-market/`, `../planetar-fce/`) |
| **Recent financial statements** | Prepare (even minimal, for a young co.) |
| **Ownership structure** | Simple (solo) — document it |
| **Management & technical team credentials** | Strong — PhD CS/ML; cite patents as *named inventor* (US 10,936,582 B2 / US 11,442,952 B2 — **background, Salesforce-assigned, never "owned"**) |
| **Project description / technical plan** | Adapt from IDEaS CH13 spine (`../../planetar/`) + the fusion-model thesis (`recenter-learned-fusion.md`) |

## What to say on the first call (positioning)

Keep it to a crisp, fundable framing:

- **Who:** Incorporated Canadian tech SME in Victoria, BC; founder is a PhD in CS/ML.
- **What:** Building **planetar — a learned cross-modal AI fusion model** for maritime
  domain awareness (resolving a vessel's identity across AIS/SAR/EO/acoustic/RF/text even
  when its AIS beacon is dark).
- **Why it's IRAP-shaped:** novel **deep-learning** R&D (→ **AI Assist**) with **defence &
  dual-use** application addressing CAF maritime-surveillance needs (→ **DI Assist**).
- **Proof of execution:** already-built services (real C bus, React analyst shell, live AIS
  ingest, SAR/EO/acoustic pipelines), a published latency benchmark, and a **competitive
  IDEaS CFP6 Challenge 13 bid already filed** with DND.
- **The ask:** start with a **small first project (or a YEP hire)** to advance the fusion
  model, with a path to a larger Core/AI Assist/DI Assist project.

Mentioning the **AI Assist** and **DI Assist** streams by name signals you've done your
homework and routes you to the right internal channel.
