# 05 — Stream fit: AI Assist & DI Assist for planetar

This is the strategic heart of the IRAP angle. NRC has layered **two targeted streams** on
top of base IRAP, and **planetar sits inside both at once** — it is a deep-learning system
(AI Assist) applied to defence/maritime surveillance (DI Assist). That dual fit is unusual
and is the strongest argument for pursuing IRAP.

---

## Stream A — **AI Assist**

| | |
|---|---|
| **What** | Helps Canadian SMEs do scientific research, product development, and **testing/validation of AI technologies** — specifically **generative AI (GenAI)** and **deep learning (DL)** — and bake them into core products/services. |
| **Backing** | **$100M**, Budget 2024, over **5 years**. |
| **Mechanism** | Runs through the standard IRAP ITA + contribution model; also offers access to **knowledge, computing resources, and AI tools**. |
| **Award size** | Not separately published — uses base IRAP tiers/rates (see [`03-FUNDING-LEVELS.md`](03-FUNDING-LEVELS.md)). |
| **How to access** | Existing clients → tell your ITA; new → call 1-877-994-4727 and name "AI Assist". |

**Why planetar fits (direct):**
- The re-centered thesis is explicitly a **learned cross-modal fusion model** — six
  heterogeneous streams (AIS, SAR, EO, acoustic, RF, text) encoded into a **shared
  spatiotemporal embedding**. That is **deep learning**, the exact target of AI Assist.
- AI Assist funds **testing & validation of AI** — planetar's hard problems (calibrated
  uncertainty, explainability, dark-vessel identity resolution) are validation-heavy R&D.
- The **"computing resources / AI tools"** benefit is materially useful — training cross-modal
  models needs compute (cf. `../../planetar/docs/compute-estimate.md`, the $18K backing).

**Angle to lead with:** the **novel fusion model itself** is the science (the same framing as
IDEaS PRC-2 novelty) — *not* the message bus, *not* heuristic log-score+threshold fusion.
AI Assist wants the learned model.

---

## Stream B — **Defence Industry Assist (DI Assist)**

| | |
|---|---|
| **What** | Helps high-potential Canadian SMEs advance **made-in-Canada defence and dual-use technologies**; provides **advice + funding** for R&D that **addresses Canadian Armed Forces (CAF) capability needs**. Strengthens Canada's defence industrial base. |
| **Backing** | **$244.2M**, starting **FY2025-26** (announced Jan 2026). |
| **Target tech areas** | Dual-use tech with commercial + defence application — reported areas include **cybersecurity, autonomous systems, advanced materials, communications, and surveillance.** |
| **Mechanism** | Delivered through IRAP's ITA network; SMEs become contributors to national defence priorities. |
| **Award size** | Not separately published — base IRAP tiers/rates apply. |
| **How to access** | Via your ITA / the toll-free line; name "DI Assist" and the CAF maritime-domain-awareness angle. |

**Why planetar fits (direct):**
- **Surveillance** and **autonomous systems** are named target areas. planetar is **maritime
  surveillance / domain awareness** — detecting **dark vessels** (those that have gone dark on
  AIS) is a textbook CAF/maritime-security capability need.
- It is **genuinely dual-use:** the same fusion model serves commercial maritime analytics
  (port security, fisheries, insurance, shipping) *and* defence surveillance.
- **Made-in-Canada:** Victoria, BC firm; Canadian IP; benefit-to-Canada is clean.
- **DND already engaged:** planetar's **IDEaS CFP6 Challenge 13** bid (filed, `CP6-132484`)
  is direct evidence the technology maps to a stated DND/CAF challenge. That is exactly the
  "addresses CAF capability needs" test DI Assist applies — and it shows DND validation.

**Note on stacking IDEaS + DI Assist:** They are different instruments (IDEaS = a DND
innovation *contract* for a specific challenge; DI Assist/IRAP = a *contribution* toward the
firm's R&D). They can coexist, but **the same eligible dollar can't be funded twice** and the
~75% total-government-assistance cap applies. The ITA will manage this — be transparent about
the IDEaS award when scoping.

---

## The combined pitch (one sentence)

> *planetar is a **made-in-Canada deep-learning fusion model** (AI Assist) for **maritime
> dark-vessel surveillance addressing CAF domain-awareness needs** (DI Assist), built by an
> incorporated Victoria tech SME with a filed IDEaS bid and working software — seeking IRAP
> support to advance the model from prototype toward a commercial, dual-use product.*

This lands in **both** priority streams, which is the rare position that makes an ITA lean in.
