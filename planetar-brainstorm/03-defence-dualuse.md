# 03 — Defence & dual-use

Canada's defence-tech funding expanded sharply in 2026 (first **Defence Industrial
Strategy**, Feb 2026; ~$6B in vehicles). Most of it is built for the dual-use,
deep-tech, software-backbone profile planetar fits — *as long as the deliverable stays
software/AI/data and you pick calls with a software-essential outcome* (the lesson from
both no-bids). Honest TRL stays 1–3.

---

## IDEaS — DND/CAF (the channel already in use) ★ proven fit

planetar's live bid (CH13 "Multi-modal AI for advanced situational decisions",
Component 1a, ≤$250K, ≤6 mo, TRL 1–3) is here. Keep it warm:

- **Competitive Projects** standing CFP umbrella **W7714-248676** — new challenges post
  under it. Component 1a (TRL 1–3 ≤$250K) is *the* honest planetar lane.
- **MINERVA Initiative** (Canadian Army) — "True North Precision" was challenge #1
  (we no-bid: hardware). **More are expected**; watch for a software/AI-essential one.
- **Eligibility includes individuals** — no incorporation needed (unlike IRAP/ISC).
- **Action:** standing watch on the IDEaS challenge list + MINERVA; bid any challenge
  whose *essential* outcomes are data fusion / entity resolution / MDA / decision support.
- https://www.canada.ca/en/department-national-defence/programs/defence-ideas.html

## NATO DIANA — Defence Innovation Accelerator for the North Atlantic ★★ strong fit

Challenge-based accelerator for **dual-use deep tech** across 32 NATO nations.

- **What you get:** contractual (non-dilutive grant-style) funding + access to **16
  accelerator sites + 200+ test centres**, plus a serious credibility stamp.
- **Scale & odds:** 2026 cohort = **150 innovators from ~3,700 submissions** (~4%);
  **~20–22 of them Canadian** — Canada competes well here.
- **Challenge areas** that fit planetar: *advanced communications & contested EM
  environments*, *autonomy & unmanned systems*, *critical infrastructure & logistics*,
  *sensing/data*. A maritime multi-sensor fusion + dark-vessel re-ID pitch maps cleanly.
- **Cadence:** annual challenge call (2026 selections ran Dec 2025 → Jan 2026 start);
  **the next call is the thing to catch** — get on the mailing list now and prep early.
- **Action:** subscribe to DIANA challenge announcements; pre-draft the dual-use pitch
  so you can move when the call opens. Higher prestige than most Canadian grants.
- https://www.diana.nato.int/

## BDC StrongNorth Fund + Catalyst Innovation Fund — ⚠ dilutive (equity/VC)

Part of Canada's 2026 defence package — *capital*, not grants.

- **StrongNorth Fund** — **$300M** dedicated VC fund for deep-tech / dual-use; expects
  to back **30–40 startups**, cheques **$1M–$20M**. This is equity — relevant only if/when
  you decide to raise and scale, not for early non-dilutive bootstrapping.
- **Catalyst Innovation Fund** — earlier-stage investment capital in the same package.
- **Note for later:** if planetar ever raises, a dedicated dual-use fund that *expects*
  long government-sales timelines is a far better fit than a generic SaaS VC. Park it.
- https://www.bdc.ca/en/bdc-capital/venture-capital/funds/defence-sector-solutions

## Regional Defence Investment Initiative (RDII) — via RDAs ★ BC-reachable

- **$357.7M over 3 yr (from 2025–26)** delivered through Regional Development Agencies —
  in BC that's **PacifiCan** — to integrate businesses into defence supply chains.
- **Why it matters:** a *regional, BC-local* defence cheque you can pursue through the
  same PacifiCan relationship as the civilian programs in [`01-canada-non-dilutive.md`](01-canada-non-dilutive.md).
- **Action:** ask PacifiCan about RDII eligibility for a dual-use software firm.
- https://www.canada.ca/en/pacific-economic-development/services/funding/regional-defence-investment-initiative.html

## Canada–EU SAFE (Security Action for Europe) — supplier access ★ longer-horizon

- Canada is the **first non-European country** with preferential access to the EU's SAFE
  program (agreement signed **2026-02-14**) — Canadian firms can be **suppliers in
  SAFE-funded procurement**.
- **Relevance:** a route to *European defence procurement* once planetar is a sellable
  capability — not an early grant, but a market-access lever worth tracking.

## Creative Destruction Lab — Defence Stream — ⚠ no cash

- CDL runs an intensive mentorship + investor-intro program (now with a **Defence
  Stream**). **Provides no cash** — mentorship and investor access only.
- **Use case:** worth it *if* you want investor relationships and structured mentorship
  while raising; not a funding source itself. 2026/27 applications open.

---

## The dual-use posture (keep it honest)

planetar is legitimately dual-use: the same fusion/re-ID backbone serves **illegal-fishing
enforcement, search-and-rescue, and pollution response** as much as defence MDA. That's
the truthful framing — and it's also what lets you pursue ocean/civilian funders
([`04-ocean-maritime.md`](04-ocean-maritime.md)) and defence funders with the *same*
codebase. Lead each application with the matching face; never overclaim TRL or hardware.
