**Subject:** Open maritime situational-awareness platform — exploring use of ONC data + a possible collaboration

Hi `[TODO: name]`,

I'm Steven Ness, a UVic CS PhD (2013) now building **planetar**, an open-source platform
that fuses real-time maritime sensor streams — AIS, satellite radar, cameras, and
**hydrophone acoustics** — into a single live picture for ocean situational awareness
(think IUU-fishing detection, search-and-rescue, and marine-mammal / pollution
monitoring).

ONC is the reason I'm reaching out. My earliest published work was hydrophone
event-detection on **NEPTUNE Canada** data (Sattar, Driessen, Tzanetakis, Ness, Page,
IEEE PacRim 2011 — 95% accuracy on the Naxys hydrophone stream), and I spent years on
large-scale orca-call analysis (the Orchive / OrcaLab archive, ORCA-SLANG at Interspeech
2021). planetar's acoustic detector is a direct descendant of that line of work — a
CAR-FAC cochlear front-end into a vessel/biological-sound classifier.

Two things I'd love to explore:

1. **Data.** Could planetar consume ONC hydrophone / observatory streams as a live input
   for vessel-acoustic cueing? I'm interested in what's available for research use and how
   you'd prefer it accessed.
2. **Collaboration.** There may be a natural research collaboration here (e.g., a
   **Mitacs-funded** student project on hydrophone vessel-detection or dark-vessel
   re-identification). I'd value a short conversation about whether something like that
   fits ONC's interests.

The platform is open-source and already working on live Victoria-area data; I'm happy to
send a one-pager or give a short demo. Would you be open to a 30-minute call in the next
couple of weeks?

Thanks very much,

Steven Ness, PhD
CEO, Zax Analytics · sness@sness.net · https://sness.net · https://planetar.ca
