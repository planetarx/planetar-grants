# 11 — Scan: Middle East funding, challenges and soft-landing programmes (2026-11-03)

**Question asked:** which Middle East competitions, prizes, accelerators, soft-landing programmes
and government innovation calls are open now (deadline after 2026-11-03) or opening by about
2027-05, and which could Zax Analytics (Canadian corporation, solo founder, GPLv3 + paid
services) realistically enter with planetar?

**Snapshot date: 2026-11-03.** Facts tagged **[V]** were read on the funder's own page on
2026-11-03. Facts tagged **[U]** come from search snippets, press coverage or aggregators and were
not confirmed on a primary page. Several primary sites (onegiantleap.com, innovationisrael.org.il,
thegarage.sa, eurekanetwork.org, support-qatar.websummit.com) returned HTTP 403 to automated
fetches, so their facts stay **[U]** until read in a browser. Re-verify before acting.

**Scope.** UAE, Saudi Arabia, Qatar, Bahrain, Oman, Kuwait, Israel, Jordan, Egypt, Türkiye. No US
funders. 32 candidates screened.

## Summary

The Middle East has **no open, non-dilutive, no-relocation grant that a Canadian AI company can
enter today**. Every route found is either (a) an accelerator that takes equity or requires a
founder to relocate or plan a local expansion, (b) a pitch competition attached to a trade show
where you must buy an exhibitor package, or (c) a government or port challenge that has no live
call. The best picks are: **Hub71 Access, Cohort 21** (Abu Dhabi; deadline "February 2027" [V];
AED 250K cash on an uncapped SAFE plus AED 250K in kind; a founder must relocate), the strongest
real option if relocation is acceptable at all. **KAUST TAQADAM** (Saudi; up to US$140K, no
equity term stated; international startups need "a clear, realistic and obtainable plan to expand
to Saudi" [V]; next window not posted, the 2026 window closed in June [U]). **Dubai Future
Accelerators** (0% equity, flights and accommodation paid, a pilot with a Dubai government body
[V]), but no current challenge was found, so it is a watch. **A Canada–Israel industrial R&D call
through NRC IRAP**, structurally the best fit (non-dilutive, no relocation), but none is open for
2026; it needs an Israeli partner and NRC IRAP client status, which is on hold. **Web Summit Qatar
2027 PITCH** (Doha, 2027-01-31 → 02-03 [V]) is the cheapest visibility play. Nothing found is
maritime-specific: the Gulf port operators (AD Ports, DP World, Mawani, Asyad) have no open startup
calls. The one recent maritime-security call, Ashdod Port's, closed 2026-06-29 [U].

## Notes on ★★ items

**1. Hub71 Access, Cohort 21.**
- *Pitch:* planetar as the open data-fusion platform for Gulf ports, logistics and smart-city
  operators. The Hormuz operating area is concrete proof of regional data. Use the
  civilian/infrastructure face (D3).
- *Catch:* this is a relocation programme, not a grant. A founder must be in Abu Dhabi for the
  first three months and "commit to relocating long-term and building a team out of Abu Dhabi".
  You must set up an ADGM subsidiary, and the cash is equity on an uncapped SAFE. Cohort 18 was
  27 startups from 2,453 applications (published 2026-06-04 [V]), and none were maritime. Treat
  it as a company-strategy decision, not a funding line.

**2. KAUST TAQADAM.**
- *Pitch:* SAR + AIS dark-vessel analytics for the Red Sea (KAUST sits on it), a Saudi
  pilot-customer plan, and the GPLv3 hosting/services model.
- *Catch:* you need a credible Saudi expansion plan and repeated travel to Thuwal. The next
  window is unannounced; the 2026 cycle ran May–June [U], so expect about 2027-05. Email
  Apply.Taqadam@kaust.edu.sa or register interest now. Equity terms are not stated on the page:
  confirm them before applying.

**3. Dubai Future Accelerators.**
- *Pitch:* only against a published challenge from a Dubai body (Dubai Maritime Authority, RTA,
  Dubai Police marine, DP World) on data fusion, maritime traffic or AI governance.
- *Catch:* no current challenge list was found. The cohort record on dubaifuture.ae ends at
  Cohort 7 (2020), so the programme's cadence is uncertain.

**4. Canada–Israel industrial R&D (NRC IRAP).**
- *Pitch:* a joint project with an Israeli maritime-tech company (the Haifa/Ashdod port
  ecosystem) on cross-modal re-identification.
- *Catch:* no 2026 call exists. It requires an Israeli partner and NRC IRAP client status, and
  `planetar-irap/` is ON HOLD. It also carries reputational and political sensitivity.

**8. QRDI Open Innovation Program.**
- *Pitch:* a Qatari-entity challenge on ports, maritime safety or national data platforms.
- *Catch:* no live call was found. Past calls targeted chatbots and utilities.

## Ruled out

- **QSTP XLR8 (Qatar):** all team members must be Qatar residents; not open to existing private
  companies [U].
- **QIA Fund of Funds (Qatar):** a programme for VC fund managers, not an application route for
  startups.
- **AD Ports Group IBTIKAR (UAE):** an ideas scheme for employees, customers and stakeholders, not
  a startup call [V innovation page 2026-11-03].
- **DP World (UAE):** no open call. Recent schemes were student challenges or India-only
  start-up series [U].
- **Mawani / Saudi LogisTech Challenge:** no Mawani startup call found. LogisTech was a 2024 MCIT
  call; status and foreign eligibility unknown [U].
- **Asyad Group (Oman):** the 2021 global challenge was one-off; the 2024 Asyad Accelerator is
  for local Omani startups [U].
- **Oman (Eleventh Station, OTF, ITHCA):** for Omani startups [U].
- **Bahrain EDB / Tamkeen:** setup support and visas for relocating founders; Tamkeen funding is
  for Bahraini entities [U].
- **UAE Space Agency / Arab Youth Space Hackathon:** Arab nationals only; closed 2026-09-25 [U].
- **ASPIRE / MBZIRC (Abu Dhabi):** no new maritime grand challenge announced. The 2023 maritime
  challenge was a robotics/hardware competition [U].
- **Dubai World Challenge for Self-Driving Transport 2027:** road autonomy; finalists due
  2026-11 [U].
- **Dubai Maritime City Authority:** no current innovation call (Innovation Quay dates from 2017)
  [U].
- **SDAIA / Blossom DominAite (Saudi):** Saudi-ecosystem AI programme; international eligibility
  unconfirmed; no deadline found [U].
- **CIIRDF Ontario–Israel / Quebec–Israel:** province-restricted (Zax is in BC) [U].
- **TÜBİTAK Public AI Ecosystem 2026 call (Türkiye):** national programme for Turkish
  applicants [U].
- **TEKNOFEST (Türkiye):** team competitions (USV, underwater) built around hardware; the USV
  deadline was 2026-02-20 [U].
- **UpLink Regenerative Blue Economy Challenge (Saudi-funded, WEF-run):** 2024 edition closed; no
  Saudi-funded 2026 ocean call found [U].
- **Zayed Sustainability Prize:** categories (health, food, energy, water, climate action)
  reward deployed impact; planetar does not fit without a stretch.
- **GITEX Global 2026 Supernova (Dubai, October):** passed. GITEX Asia and Africa editions are
  outside region scope.
- **Kuwait, Jordan, Egypt:** no programme open to foreign companies was found in this pass. These
  countries were only lightly searched, so treat this as "not covered", not "nothing exists".

**Sensitivity (one line):** maritime surveillance of the Gulf and Hormuz, and dual-use port
security in Israel, are politically sensitive end uses. Before any defence or security
engagement, check Canada's Export Control List and permit requirements, and keep the
civilian/open-infrastructure face (D3) for Gulf applications.
