# 10 — European funding scan for a Canadian AI company (2026-11-03)

**Question asked:** which European competitions, challenges, prizes, accelerators and calls that
are open now (deadline after 2026-11-03) or open within about six months could Zax Analytics
(Victoria, BC; solo founder; planetar, GPLv3) realistically enter?

**Snapshot date: 2026-11-03.** Facts marked "verified" were read on the primary page named in the
Verified column on that date. Anything else is tagged **UNVERIFIED**. Horizon Europe dates and
budgets come from the EU Funding & Tenders Portal topic records (`topicDetails/<id>.json`, the data
behind each topic page). Those records give dates, not times; Horizon Europe deadlines are normally
17:00 Brussels time, so confirm the time on the topic page before submitting.

**Out of scope:** US funders, and the items already covered in [`07`](07-funding-radar.md) and
[`08`](08-procurement-scan-2026-10-06.md): NLnet/Restack (incl. `HORIZON-CL4-2025-03-DATA-11`),
`HORIZON-CL3-2026-01-BM-01`, `HORIZON-CL3-2026-01-DRS-03`, EMSA BDW, the DIANA 2027 multidomain
sensing challenge, NCIA eAirC2, ESA InCubed, Canada–EU SAFE, and the EMSA platform benchmark.

## Eligibility baseline (applies to every row below)

- **Horizon Europe Pillar II (Clusters 1–6, Missions):** Canada has been associated since
  2024-07-03. Canadian entities receive EU grants directly, **but only inside a consortium**:
  "Every consortium must include at least one partner from an EU member state and at least two
  additional partners, each from different countries of EU member states and/or associated
  countries" (ISED Pillar 2 page, modified 2026-02-19). In 2026–27, several AI and space topics
  restrict participation for strategic-autonomy reasons, and those topics **name Canada** in the
  list of eligible countries (checked below topic by topic).
- **Horizon Europe Pillar III (EIC):** Canada is not associated. For the EIC Accelerator,
  "In general Canadian entities are not eligible ... an exception exists ... to relocate their
  company to an EU Member State", completed before the full application. EIC Pathfinder and
  Transition: Canadians can join only as unfunded third-country participants (ISED Pillar 3 page,
  modified 2026-02-19).
- **ESA:** Canada is a Cooperating State (agreement runs to 2030-01-01). "Canadian organizations
  are allowed to bid on tenders for ESA contract related to activities and programs in which Canada
  participates." Bids under ARTES 4.0, GSTP and NAVISP need a CSA letter of support. For any other
  programme Canada is in, a Canadian company can respond to ESA directly. Canada participates in
  Copernicus Space Component, FutureEO, European Earth Watch (incl. InCubed), **European Resilience
  from Space (ERS)**, E3P, NAVISP and others. Under ARTES 4.0 it is **not** in Business
  Applications – Space Solutions or Partnership Projects. That notice predates the November 2025
  Ministerial and CSA says it will be updated (CSA pages, read 2026-11-03).
- **Eurostars:** Canada is a Eurostars country, but not an EU or Horizon-associated one for
  Eurostars purposes. A Canadian SME can lead a project, but the consortium must include an entity
  from at least one EU Member State or Horizon Europe associated country. Canadian costs are funded
  by NRC IRAP (eligibility guidelines via search snippet: **UNVERIFIED** on the current version).
- **EU defence funds (EDF, EDIP and their cascade calls):** participants must be established in the
  EU or an EDF-associated country, and must not be controlled from a non-associated third country
  (EDF FSTP text, read on the portal). SAFE is the only Canada carve-out and is already tracked.

## 1. Summary

The realistic European routes for a one-person Canadian company are **partner seats in
consortium calls**, plus a few UK and ESA routes that admit non-domestic applicants. No
single-applicant European grant was found that a Canadian company can win on its own except
ARIA, which funds non-UK teams only when they show UK benefit. **Top picks:**

1. **Eurostars Call 12** (opens 2026-12-17, closes **2027-03-04 14:00 CET**). This is the only
   SME-led R&D grant where a Canadian company can be lead applicant. It needs one EU or associated
   SME partner, such as a Nordic or Dutch maritime or SAR company, and the Canadian costs are
   funded by IRAP.
2. **Horizon Europe `HORIZON-CL3-2027-01-BM-01`**, the open topic for border management
   (opens 2027-05-05, closes **2027-11-04**). It explicitly covers "civilian maritime security".
   This is the 2027 successor to the BM-01 call already being tracked, and the best thematic match
   for dark-vessel fusion. It is consortium-only and needs practitioners from at least two
   Member States or associated countries.
3. **Horizon Europe `HORIZON-CL4-2027-SPACE-03-33`**, digital enablers for collaborative EO and
   SatCom (opens 2027-03-09, closes **2027-09-02**, EUR 1.5–3M per grant). This restricted space
   topic **names Canada** as eligible, and its scope includes on-ground and in-space EO processing
   and EO service security, where the SAR detector and provenance work fit.
4. **ARIA Scaling Trust, Tracks 2+3** (rolling, reviewed quarterly, GBP 200k–2M for Track 2).
   The Track 2 scope is open-source tooling for verifiable multi-agent requirements and protocols,
   the nearest European fit for the AI-assurance and provenance line. Non-UK teams can be funded
   only with a UK-benefit plan. The next cut-off date is not published.
5. **ESA ERS-EO** (European Resilience from Space, Earth observation), which Canada subscribed to
   in 2026: a watch item, not a dated call. It is ESA's security and ISR EO line, and Canada's
   subscription means Canadian firms can bid on its tenders. No maritime-specific tender was found
   yet (**UNVERIFIED**).

Everything else is either a poor thematic fit, closed to Canadians, or too far off to date.

## 3. Notes on ★★★ / ★★ items

**#1 Eurostars Call 12.**
- *Pitch:* a two-company project: learned SAR↔AIS dark-vessel re-identification, with planetar as
  the open fusion layer and an EU SME supplying the data, sensor or customer channel. Candidate
  partner types: a Norwegian or Danish AIS/SAR analytics firm, or a Dutch or Belgian port-security
  integrator. Eurostars is TRL 4–6, market-led, with a commercial plan due within two years of the
  project's end, which suits the paid-services model.
- *Catch:*
  - Partner-finding has to start now; four months is tight.
  - IRAP funding is a separate national decision.
  - The EU partner's national funder must also say yes.
  - xView3-trained weights are CC BY-NC-SA and cannot be the commercial deliverable.
- *Action:* contact an NRC IRAP ITA in November 2026.

**#2 `HORIZON-CL3-2027-01-BM-01`.**
- *Pitch:* multi-sensor dark-vessel re-identification with per-message provenance, framed around
  fundamental-rights-preserving maritime border security (the topic's required framing).
- *Catch:*
  - Consortium-only, with practitioner requirements; a solo founder can only be a work-package
    partner.
  - About two grants of ~EUR 4M will be awarded, so competition will be heavy.
  - The call text pushes intersectional and fundamental-rights analysis, so a social-science
    partner is needed.
  - Do not let the CH14 provenance thesis bleed into a CH13-style fusion pitch, or the reverse.
- *Action:* use the 2026 BM-01 consortium contacts (already tracked) to get onto a 2027 bid before
  the call opens on 2027-05-05.

**#3 `HORIZON-CL4-2027-SPACE-03-33`.**
- *Pitch:* a learned Sentinel-1 ship-detection and processing building block, plus provenance-
  tracked EO data delivery for the "security of ... EO services" outcome.
- *Catch:*
  - The topic leans to SatCom–EO synergy and payload/processing technology; an analytics-only
    contribution is a minor part.
  - Restricted calls involve ownership and control checks.
  - The grants are small (EUR 1.5–3M) and shared across a consortium.

**#4 `HORIZON-CL4-2027-04-DATA-03`.**
- *Pitch:* the provenance bus and envelope (UUIDv7, HLC, lineage) as the data-consistency and
  traceability layer for decentralised AI pipelines across edge and cloud.
- *Catch:*
  - Two very large grants (~EUR 17.5M each) will go to big infrastructure consortia, so planetar
    would be a small partner at best.
  - The topic is about compute continuum and energy, not maritime.

**#5 ARIA Scaling Trust.**
- *Pitch:* open-source, auditable message and provenance infrastructure that lets agents prove
  what they were told and what they decided: a reusable component for the Arena.
- *Catch:*
  - The thesis is multi-agent negotiation and verification; planetar's provenance substrate is
    adjacent, not central.
  - A non-UK award needs a concrete UK contribution plan.
  - ARIA "may choose not to make awards in a given review cycle".

**#7 ESA ERS-EO.**
- *Pitch:* maritime ISR analytics (SAR detection + AIS correlation) as an information-layer
  service.
- *Catch:*
  - No tender found yet.
  - ERS is security-sensitive, so Canadian access to individual elements may be constrained even
    with a subscription.
  - The ALPSTAR space-segment element was for its own participating states.
- *Action:* email `esa_programme-esa_program@asc-csa.gc.ca` asking which ERS-EO elements Canada
  subscribed to and whether ground-segment and analytics ITTs are open to Canadian bidders.

**#8 ESA EO Science for Society open call.**
- *Pitch:* a small ESA activity on open dark-vessel or whale-strike-risk analytics from Sentinel-1
  plus AIS.
- *Catch:* the call's 2026 status is unverified. Read the "Open Call Innovation" page on
  eo4society.esa.int before investing time.

**#9 NATO DIANA 2028.**
- *Pitch:* the same as the 2027 drafts in [`../planetar-diana/`](../planetar-diana/).
- *Catch:* the date is inferred. Re-check `diana.nato.int` in May 2027.

## 4. Ruled out

- **`HORIZON-CL3-2026-01-SSRI-01`, open topic on disruptive civil-security innovation:** closes
  2026-11-05, two days away. No time to join a consortium.
- **Sustainable Blue Economy Partnership, 4th joint call:** pre-proposals were due 2026-11-16
  15:00 CET (full proposals ~2027-06-14), budget ~EUR 38M. Canada is not among the 28 funding
  countries, so a Canadian partner is self-funded only and does not count toward the minimum
  consortium.
- **NATO SPS call 2027-1** (full proposals 2026-11-15 23:59 CET): "For-profit private companies are
  not eligible" to be Co-Directors.
- **EIC Accelerator:** Canada is not associated to Pillar III; the only route is relocating to an EU
  Member State first (ISED).
- **EIC Pathfinder / Transition:** Canadians can join only without EU funding (ISED).
- **European Defence Fund, EDIP, and EDF cascade calls** (e.g., BattleVerse open call, closes
  2026-11-16; `EDF-EDIP-P-2027-FNLC-CPA-C5ISRS`, closes 2027-02-16): participants must be
  established in the EU or EDF-associated countries, with no non-associated third-country control.
- **Digital Europe Programme / ECCC** (e.g., `DIGITAL-ECCC-2027-DEPLOY-CYBER-11-DUALUSE`, closes
  2027-01-14): Canada is not a DEP associated country. The Commission FAQ lists EEA, Western
  Balkans, Ukraine, Moldova, Türkiye and Switzerland.
- **UK Defence Innovation, Innovation in Support of Operations cycles:** UK-registered
  organisations doing the work in the UK only (cycle 8 closed 2026-09-22).
- **UK AISI Alignment Project / Challenge Fund / Systemic Safety:** all closed. The Alignment
  Project says a similar programme is "unlikely in 2026". The Challenge Fund admitted non-UK
  academics and non-profits, not companies.
- **SPRIND Next Frontier AI:** closed 2026-06-01; open to teams "from across Europe". Not Canada.
- **ESA Business Applications – Space Solutions**, incl. the 4S "AIS RF emissions" maritime
  surveillance opportunity: Canada is not in that ARTES 4.0 line per the CSA notice. Re-check after
  CSA updates the page post-CM25.
- **ESA BIC incubators:** each centre requires local residency or incorporation (e.g., Latvia,
  Estonia, Switzerland). Possible only through an EU subsidiary.
- **Innowwide (Eureka) Call 5** (closes 2026-12-01): applicants must be SMEs in the EU or listed
  European countries. A Canadian company could only be a non-EU subcontractor to such an SME.
- **NATO Innovation Fund:** Canada has pledged but is not a limited partner (secondary reporting).
  NIF is equity, not a call.
- **CASSINI Challenges 2026:** closed 2026-03-26; no 2027 round found.
- **Copernicus Masters:** no 2026 edition found.
- **Prince Albert II of Monaco Foundation:** the 2026 session ran 2026-06-01 to 06-30 (climate,
  biodiversity, water); no ocean-specific call is open.
- **Canada–UK Ocean Collaborative R&D** (Innovate UK + Ocean Supercluster): the last round closed
  2023-10-18; no 2026 round found. Worth watching, since its themes included "ocean sensors and
  environmental monitoring".
- **Frontier-model topics** (`...DIGITAL-EMERGING-11`, `HORIZON-BRIDGING-2027-01-13`): Canada is
  eligible, but each needs a frontier AI lab's compute.
