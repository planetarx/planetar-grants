# 02 — Open-source-native funders

These fund open-source work *because* it's open. Several accept **individuals** (no
incorporation needed) — making this the **unblocked path** while Zax Analytics'
corporate status is sorted. They require a free/open license, which planetar already is.

---

## NLnet Foundation — **Restack** fund ★★ best first move

European public-interest funder for open internet/digital-commons tech. Light,
no-nonsense application; pays for concrete deliverables.

> **⚠️ Corrected 2026-06-03.** The **NGI Zero Commons Fund is CLOSED** — its 13th and
> final call was 2026-06-01 and the NGI0 budget is fully allocated. NGI Zero **Core** is
> also closed. The successor is **Restack**, NLnet's new Horizon Europe "Open Internet
> Stack" cascade-funding programme — same NGI Zero bottom-up model, same amounts/eligibility.
> The currently-open *other* NLnet funds (NGI TALER = payments, NGI Fediversity = hosting,
> Open Social = ActivityPub) are **off-theme** for planetar — don't force-fit them.

> **⚠️ Re-verified 2026-07-02 — the 2026-08-01 window is NOT usable.** Restack itself is
> confirmed real (announced 2026-01-31: Horizon Europe "Open Internet Stack", €10M,
> €5K–50K grants, rolling calls every ~2 months June 2026 → 2030, individuals eligible) —
> **but its calls have not opened yet.** As of 2026-07-02, `nlnet.nl/propose` offers **only
> NGI TALER and NGI Fediversity** (deadline 2026-08-01) — no Restack option, and **no
> general Open Call either** ("our regular open call will reopen after the summer").
> **New action:** hold the drafted application; **check https://nlnet.nl/propose/ monthly**
> and submit to the first Restack / regular call that appears (expect fall 2026).

- **Fund:** **Restack** — *"a healthy Open Internet Stack… open, resilient, trustworthy
  digital infrastructure… middleware without vendor lock-in… local-first infrastructure."*
- **Amount:** **€5,000–€50,000** per project; **scale-up** track for proven projects.
- **Cadence:** **rolling open calls every ~2 months → 2030** *per the Restack announcement* —
  but **the first call had not opened as of 2026-07-02**; NLnet's regular call reopens
  **after summer 2026**. (The 2026-08-01 deadline is Taler/Fediversity only.)
- **Eligible:** free/open hardware / **software** / data / standards. **Individuals and
  orgs of any type** — no incorporation required. Global applicants.
- **Bonus services:** free security & accessibility audits, mentoring, testing,
  licensing advice — valuable for a solo founder.
- **planetar angle:** pitch the **bus + zmesg envelope as open infrastructure** — "an
  open, low-latency, auditable messaging substrate for multi-sensor data, with an open
  typed-envelope standard (UUIDv7, ns timestamps, provenance)." That's *exactly* Restack's
  "middleware without vendor lock-in / Open Internet Stack" language. *Frame as open infra,
  not a defence product.* Draft ready: [`drafts/01-nlnet-application.md`](drafts/01-nlnet-application.md).
- **Action:** hold until the Restack / regular call opens (check monthly; expect fall 2026),
  then submit immediately. Still the **lowest-friction non-dilutive money on this whole
  list** — just not an August cheque.
- Apply: https://nlnet.nl/propose/ (Call Selection = **Restack**, fallback **Open Call**) ·
  Form fields are mirrored in [`drafts/01`](drafts/01-nlnet-application.md).
- **Other coming-soon NLnet funds to watch:** **CodeSupply** (cybersecurity / software
  supply chain) and **ELFA** (decentralized encrypted collaboration).

## Sovereign Tech Agency (Germany) ★ for critical OSS infrastructure

Funds *critical* open-source infrastructure that scales across many sectors. Globally
open, no geographic restriction. Three relevant programs:

- **Sovereign Tech Fund** — invests in maintenance/development of core OSS components
  that underpin the digital ecosystem. Bar is "critical infrastructure many depend on" —
  a *stretch* for planetar today, but a target once the bus/envelope has real downstream
  users. Keep on the 12-month horizon.
- **Sovereign Tech Fellowship** — pays maintainers for maintenance + community +
  documentation. Freelance **3–12 mo, 6–32 h/wk**, or 2-yr employment. (2026 round
  applications closed ~Apr 6; watch for the next.)
- **Sovereign Tech Standards** network — €4,800–€5,200/mo for ~10 h/wk contributing to
  **IETF/W3C/ISO** standards work. If the **zmesg envelope** is pushed as an open
  standard (e.g., an IETF draft for a sensor-message envelope), this could pay for that
  standardization effort. (2026 call closed ~May 19; watch for re-opening.)
- **Action:** not eligible-today for the Fund, but **build toward it** — grow real
  users, and consider standardizing zmesg (which also opens the Standards program).
- https://www.sovereign.tech/

## GitHub Sponsors + Open Collective ★ recurring, audience-driven

The community-revenue rails. Detail in [`05-community-revenue.md`](05-community-revenue.md);
summary here because they're OSS-funding infrastructure:

- **GitHub Sponsors** — recurring/one-time sponsorship via a button on the repo. Scoped
  tiers ("48-hr priority issue response") convert far better than "support my work."
  Documented solo ranges **$800–$4,000/mo** for projects with a real audience.
- **Open Collective** (via **Open Source Collective** fiscal host) — transparent
  community treasury; can receive corporate sponsorships and the GitHub Sponsors payout
  (≈10% admin fee). Lets a company expense-sponsor you without a contract.
- **Action:** add `FUNDING.yml` + a Sponsor button the day the repo goes public; stand up
  an Open Collective so companies *can* pay you. This is the floor of the flywheel.

## Others to keep on radar

- **Linux Foundation** (+ sub-foundations like LF Edge / LF AI & Data) — hosting,
  governance, sometimes funding for projects that join a foundation. Relevant *if*
  planetar grows a multi-contributor community; premature solo.
- **Sloan Foundation** — funds open-source *scientific* research software (e.g., "better
  software for science"). Reachable only via the research/ONC framing (hydrophone/ocean
  science) — long shot, note it.
- **Open Technology Fund (US)** — internet-freedom / censorship-circumvention focus;
  planetar isn't a fit. Skip unless the framing changes.
- **FUTO, Plurality/Gitcoin-style grants** — smaller/ideological OSS funders; low effort
  to add a Gitcoin grant during a public round, marginal $.

---

## Why start here

NLnet is the single best first move on the entire brainstorm: **non-dilutive,
individual-eligible (no incorporation), open-source-native, fast/light application,
and it bundles free audits + mentoring.** Land an NLnet grant and you have (a) money,
(b) a credibility stamp that strengthens *every* later application (IRAP, Sovereign
Tech, Ocean Supercluster), and (c) a forcing function to ship the open-infra story.
