# 04 — Ocean / maritime (blue economy)

planetar's flagship demo is maritime (AIS/SAR/EO/hydrophone/RF fusion, dark-vessel
detection). That makes Canada's well-funded **blue-economy** ecosystem a natural,
*civilian-framed* funding lane — and one where being in **Victoria, BC** is a real
advantage (ONC is next door at UVic). Lead with the civilian story: illegal fishing,
search-and-rescue, pollution/whale-strike monitoring.

## Ocean Startup Project — Startup Challenge ★★ timely, low effort

National blue-tech accelerator (ranked top-5 in North America for the blue economy).

- **Amount:** up to **$25,000** in awards per company.
- **Cohort:** up to **15 early-stage Canadian startups, TRL ≤6** — planetar's band fits.
- **⏰ OPEN NOW — closes 2026-07-26 23:59** (opened 2026-06-05; re-verified 2026-07-02).
  **Near-term, low lift** — one of the easiest concrete cheques on this list.
- **Eligibility gotcha (verified):** incorporated applicants must be **< 5 years old, < 10
  FTE, founders majority owners** → **Zax Analytics fails (age + spouse ownership); apply
  as an individual** — only *winners* must register a (new) Canadian entity. Full plan +
  draft answers: [`drafts/02`](drafts/02-ocean-startup-challenge.md); application
  workspace: [`../planetar-ocean-startup/`](../planetar-ocean-startup/).
- **Also:** the **Ocean Idea Challenge** (earlier-stage) ran 2026-02-26 → 04-12 — closed;
  watch for the 2027 round.
- **Action:** submit before **2026-07-26**; civilian MDA framing; disclose the OOR
  relationship proactively (C2).
- https://www.oceanstartupproject.ca/startup-challenge

## Schmidt Marine Technology Partners ★★★ live window, real money *(added 2026-07-02)*

"Venture-philanthropy" program of The Schmidt Family Foundation for ocean-health
technology — the closest thing on this list to a funder purpose-built for planetar's
civilian face.

- **Amount:** grants typically **US$100K–$400K**; competitive (~10% of reviewed
  proposals funded).
- **⏰ Window:** initial-proposal portal **open 2026-06-01 → 2026-07-31** (verified 2026-07-02).
- **Eligible:** universities, non-profits, and **startups, globally**; practical, scalable
  **hardware or software**.
- **Focus areas:** **sustainable fisheries**, habitat health, **ocean observing / novel
  sensing**, and services/tools that strengthen the marine-tech ecosystem.
- **planetar angle:** open-source multi-sensor fusion + dark-vessel re-identification =
  **IUU-fishing monitoring and open ocean-observing infrastructure** (the Global Fishing
  Watch problem, delivered as auditable open infrastructure). Civilian framing only (D3);
  disclose the OOR relationship (C2).
- **Action:** submit an initial proposal before **2026-07-31** — workspace:
  [`../planetar-schmidt-marine/`](../planetar-schmidt-marine/).
- https://schmidtmarine.org/ · portal: https://schmidtmarine.secure-platform.com/a

## Ocean-conservation philanthropy — the funder class behind Global Fishing Watch *(scan)*

Schmidt Marine is one member of a philanthropic funder class these files previously
didn't touch: **Oceankind, Walton Family Foundation, Packard, Pew** all fund IUU-fishing
monitoring and ocean-observation technology (they are the money behind Global Fishing
Watch). Mostly relationship/invitation-driven rather than open calls — *unverified, to
research*. A **Global Fishing Watch partnership or data integration** (open data, shared
IUU mission) would be the natural door-opener into the whole class.

## Canada's Ocean Supercluster (OSC) ★ big $, needs a consortium

Federal Global Innovation Cluster co-funding large collaborative ocean-tech projects.

- **Scale:** projects in the **$0.9M–$6M** range (e.g., **$3.1M OCEANIC** AI digital-twin
  maritime-autonomy project; $940K Amphibian UWILD; $2.5M maintenance project). OSC
  co-funds; **partners match** the rest.
- **Structure:** built for **industry-led consortia**, not solo founders — you'd join or
  assemble a project with partners (a shipping operator, a university, a sensor vendor).
- **planetar angle:** the **data-fusion / digital-twin / situational-awareness layer**
  inside a larger ocean-operations project. Note OSC already funds AI digital-twin
  maritime work (OCEANIC) — planetar is the open data fabric under exactly that.
- **Action:** medium-horizon — find a consortium to join (ONC, COVE network, a BC marine
  operator) rather than applying solo. Pairs with Mitacs for the academic partner.
- https://oceansupercluster.ca/

## Ocean Networks Canada (ONC) — UVic, Victoria ★ local strategic partner

ONC operates world-class cabled ocean observatories out of **UVic — in your city**. Not
a grant program per se, but a **strategic local partner and data source**:

- **Hydrophone & sensor data** — ONC is a premier source of the exact hydrophone/acoustic
  + oceanographic streams planetar fuses. A data/research partnership de-risks the
  hydrophone pipeline *and* creates a citable collaboration.
- **Leverage:** an ONC partnership underwrites a **Mitacs Accelerate** internship (academic
  supervisor + student), strengthens **Ocean Supercluster** and **NLnet/Sloan** (research
  framing) applications, and is a credibility anchor generally.
- **Action:** reach out for a research/data conversation — highest-value *local*
  relationship on this list, and it compounds across multiple funders.
- https://www.oceannetworks.ca/

## COAST (Victoria) — Centre for Ocean Applied Sustainable Technologies ★ local ecosystem

Victoria-based blue-economy cluster (accelerator programming, industry network) — the
*local* counterpart to Halifax's COVE, in planetar's home city. Network and ecosystem
value rather than direct cheques; pairs naturally with the ONC relationship and any
Ocean Startup / Schmidt Marine win. *Programs unverified — scan https://canadacoast.ca/
when pursuing.*

## COVE — Centre for Ocean Ventures & Entrepreneurship (Halifax) ★ ecosystem

Marine-tech innovation hub + testing ground (robotics, sensors, autonomous vessels,
subsea monitoring) on a former Coast Guard base.

- **Relevance:** Atlantic-coast hub and testbed; gateway to the broader blue-tech network,
  partners, and at-sea testing infrastructure. Less local than ONC, but the node that
  connects to Ocean Supercluster projects and Atlantic ocean-tech partners.
- **Action:** keep as a network/testbed option, especially if a project needs real
  at-sea validation.
- https://coveocean.com/

## Why the ocean lane matters strategically

It lets planetar raise money **without a defence label** — the same codebase, pitched as
open maritime safety infrastructure, reaches ocean/climate/blue-economy funders who would
never fund a weapons-adjacent product. It also concentrates around **Victoria/ONC**, your
home turf. Run this lane *in parallel* with defence (Ch.03): one repo, two funder
ecosystems, honest framing on each.
