  surveillance to coalition interoperability hubs" (CH14). Doctrine hooks: **NORAD
  modernization**, CAF Digital Campaign Plan, DND/CAF AI Strategy, Cyber Forces.

  engine — grounded in applicant's AGPL open-source spine + new Canadian foreground IP.
- **Risk reduction:** immutable, exportable lineage supports accreditation, forensic review,
  coalition releasability — reducing the cost/risk of fielding AI fusion.
- **Edge/SWaP reach:** dependency-light C engine deployable on a ruggedized laptop.
