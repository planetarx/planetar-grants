## Draft (workspace markdown — strip headings before submission)

**(a) Addresses a stated capability gap.** CH14 states the gap plainly: Canada "lacks a sovereign, Canadian-developed and Canadian-controlled compliance engine for multi-domain fusion," and classification enforcement during fusion is done today by manual review and procedural checklists that cannot keep pace with AI-fusion volume and velocity. The FCE removes that bottleneck — machine-readable policy is enforced automatically, per element, at the chokepoint between ingestion and fusion — delivering CH14's two operational benefits at once: it **reduces risk** (no un-audited or non-compliant cross-domain merge passes silently; every disposition is journalled immutably) and **increases tempo** (enforcement runs at fusion speed, without the human-approval step that gates manual review). The capability is built on the applicant's own AGPL open-source spine and net-new, Canadian-owned FCE foreground IP — a genuinely sovereign capability, replicable on commodity Linux, aligned to NORAD modernization, the CAF Digital Campaign Plan, the DND/CAF AI Strategy, and the Cyber Forces mandate.

**(b) Enhances S&T capability.** Automated, in-path, per-element compliance enforcement *during* multi-sensor fusion is a capability not present in current Canadian fusion systems. *Accreditation by construction:* exportable, replayable ingestion-to-output lineage (modelled on W3C PROV [S5]) supports compliance review, forensic analysis, and accreditation without a bolt-on reporting step. *Coalition interoperability:* classification markings structured to NATO STANAG 4774/4778 [S1, S2] let releasability and coalition caveats be enforced and shared by machine, not memo. *Operator trust:* every disposition renders its governing rule and the element's provenance for inspection, with controlled, immutably-logged override — addressing a known barrier to operational AI adoption.

**(c) Matures the field.** The FCE is, in CH14's words, "a reusable building block for every future fusion system." *Generalizable pattern:* "enforce policy at the fusion chokepoint" transfers without redesign to Joint ISR (SIGINT + EO/IR + radar), Arctic surveillance, tactical-edge dismounted, and coalition interoperability hubs — the engine is agnostic to which modalities cross the bus. *Lower barrier to accreditable AI:* by making compliance a structural property rather than an afterthought, the FCE shortens the path to authority-to-operate for fielded AI fusion. *Durable, sovereign output:* applicant-controlled open-source code and new Canadian-owned IP outlast the contract window as reference implementations and a publishable contribution to CAF multi-domain compliance.
