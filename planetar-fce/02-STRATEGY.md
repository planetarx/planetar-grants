# 02 — Strategy: sovereign compliance-by-design

## The wedge

CH14 says it plainly: *"Canada currently lacks a sovereign, Canadian-developed and
Canadian-controlled compliance engine for multi-domain fusion."* That is a **capability-gap
pitch, not a performance pitch** — the win condition is *credible sovereign Canadian IP that
enforces policy at fusion speed*, not the best model. planetar's edge is that it already
owns the fusion chokepoint and the provenance/audit primitives, so it can deliver a working
FCE in a 1a where a cold-start competitor delivers slideware.

## Three pillars (map to the scored narratives)

1. **Own the chokepoint (feasibility — PRC-4).** Not "we will build a compliance layer" but
   "the bus every fused element crosses is already ours, already provenance-stamped, already
   journalled — the FCE is enforcement at a seat we hold." Cheapest credible path to TRL 3.
2. **Compliance-by-design as the novelty (PRC-2).** Enforcement *inside* the fusion path
   driven by **machine-readable ODRL policy**, with permit/restrict/downgrade/segregate
   dispositions and ns-scale overhead — vs. today's manual review / checklists. The novelty
   is the engine + policy model, **not** the fusion model (which is background here).
3. **Sovereign + reusable building block (impact — PRC-3).** A Canadian-owned FCE is a
   reusable component "from Arctic surveillance to coalition interoperability hubs"
   (CH14's own words) — NORAD modernization, CAF Digital Campaign Plan, Cyber Forces.

## What to carry over vs. drop from the CH13 pitch

- **Carry:** provenance-native substrate; ns benchmark (re-cast as *no added enforcement
  latency*); built-not-vapour honesty spine; applicant credentials; GBA+.
- **Drop / demote:** "learned cross-modal fusion model is the thesis"; dark-vessel
  re-identification as the headline (it becomes a governed use-case / demo domain).

## Positioning risk to manage

We must not look like a maritime-fusion demo wearing a compliance hat. The narrative has to
lead with **policy enforcement, classification handling, and audit/accreditation** as the
subject — with MDA as the *setting* the demo runs in. See `THESIS.md` "Cut / demote."
